# Product review — shared-direction release

## The issue and the correction

The earlier design engine received names and selected qualities but not Qloo's returned connections. That made the graph secondary to a generic design template. It also implicitly assigned main/support/accent importance by input position. The new orchestration requires a fully checked Qloo candidate and an explicit user interpretation before a result can complete. The candidate's identity, description, per-input scores and approved transfer are now fields of the canonical plan.

The product boundary is deliberate: Qloo finds cultural common ground; the user approves how to interpret it; the catalog supplies bounded material choices. The app does not pretend that an affinity score establishes visual or material compatibility. A selected restaurant is a creative reference chosen by the user, not an automatic recommendation to copy its interior.

## Actual read-through

Read on 2026-10-28 from the final production-browser outputs:

- Compact dark dramatic room: Supreme was the selected, fully scored shared reference. The user's dramatic-form interpretation is stated separately from the brand description. Charcoal wall paint stays dark, upholstery is removable, and accent treatment is limited to small existing objects. Instructions explicitly preserve the floor instead of using wall paint on it. The compact focal form and retain-existing-pieces budget approach remain visible.
- Light calm capsule: Everlane was the selected shared reference, with individual evidence for Aesop and Bon Iver. Technical cut and closure detail are the user's interpretation; natural texture keeps its own original influence role. Garment shell, inner layer and hardware use appropriate materials and muted colors.
- Dark calm artwork: Vice was the selected shared reference. The user's cinematic interpretation is stated without claiming that Qloo measured a visual match. The tonal field and measured grid retain distinct roles. Paper/acrylic/ink instructions call for a small layer test. Dark remains independent of calm.
- The user's spontaneous three-reference test also completed: Nirvana had individual scores for all three inputs. Missing scores for some other domains were retained, not patched. The original three qualities remained distinct in the strategy and export.

The final selector shows one balanced candidate per available category, with other candidates under an expandable section. No candidate or interpretation is preselected. The selected description can be read in full. The mobile strategy screenshot is readable and stacks without horizontal overflow. Schematics remain explicitly illustrative palette studies, not measured layouts or finished designs.

## Remaining limits

Creative interpretations are limited to five choices, and the catalog is small. Some role explanations repeat. Two different shared references can produce the same materials when the user gives them the same interpretation and needs; this is disclosed behavior, not hidden visual analysis. The graph determines which shared references are available, while user decisions determine the creative transfer. The result should be judged as a grounded starting point and a small experiment, not a bespoke final design or a professional material specification.

The local product gate passes for this bounded scope. Public hosting, repository publication and deployed access remain separate delivery gates.
